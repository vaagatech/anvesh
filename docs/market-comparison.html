When to Choose Anvesh

  • Cost-sensitive workloads — you need search but can't justify $5K+/month
  • Hybrid search — you need both BM25 text and vector similarity in one system
  • Edge/IoT — you need search on resource-constrained hardware (ARM, limited RAM)
  • Data sovereignty — you must self-host and control your data
  • Startup velocity — deploy in 60 seconds, not 6 hours

When NOT to Choose Anvesh

  • Elasticsearch ecosystem — you need Kibana dashboards, Logstash pipelines, or Elastic APM
  • Petabyte scale — you have 100+ nodes and need Elastic's mature cluster management
  • Fully managed only — you want a SaaS service with zero ops (consider Elastic Cloud or Pinecone)
